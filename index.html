Chitalka Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Developer: Jason Naya

Contact: jasonnaya@live.com

Chitalka (“the app”, “we”) is a Russian adjective learning app. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not. In short: Chitalka has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, it works offline with content stored in the app, and we do not collect personal information about you.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.
  • We do not use the microphone or the camera.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

Information stored on your device

The app does not save your reading, quiz answers or any other activity on your device. Each time you open it, you start fresh.

Internet use

Chitalka’s stories, adjectives and sentences are stored inside the app. The app does not contact any server to load that content, and it does not send anything you read or answer anywhere.

The only internet activity is with the App Store, when you buy or restore the unlock described below.

Purchases

Four categories are free. The others are unlocked with one of three purchases: a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details, and we do not keep a record of your purchase. The app asks Apple whether the unlock is owned, both when you buy it and when you restore it. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

Sound

The app plays short sound effects, such as the category wheel tick and the quiz answer sounds. These sounds are stored inside the app. The app does not record or store anything you hear.

Children

Chitalka is a general-audience educational app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data security

Because the app does not keep any data about you, there is no personal data stored by us to protect. Your device’s own security, such as a passcode and device encryption, still applies to the device itself.

Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time. You can control sound through your device settings. To stop a monthly or annual subscription, cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings (on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing or in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: jasonnaya@live.com
