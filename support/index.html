Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what is paid?

Size & Shape, Strength & Weight, Weather & Seasons and Colors & Light are free. The other categories are unlocked with a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. A tap on a locked category opens the unlock screen with all three choices.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap a locked category to open the unlock screen, then tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

The monthly and annual plans renew automatically until they are cancelled. Cancel them in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Chitalka. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase is a single payment and does not renew.

I was charged but the categories are still locked.

Tap Restore purchase first. If that does not help, email me with the date of the purchase. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Yes. The stories, adjectives, quiz and sounds are all stored in the app, so it works offline.

How do I choose a category?

Swipe the category wheel sideways, or tap a category word to bring it to the middle. The stories in that category then open in the reader.

How do I read a story?

  • Swipe the page sideways to move to the next or previous page.
  • The adjectives on the current page are shown at the top.
  • Swipe the text up or down to make it bigger or smaller. The reader stays on the same sentence.

How does the quiz work?

Tap Quiz at the top right of the reader. Each question shows a sentence with one blank. Choose the correct form of the adjective, then move on to the next question. At the end you see your score and which answers you got wrong.

There is a mistake in a story, translation or sentence.

Please email me the adjective and the sentence. The stories were written for the app and may contain errors or sound unnatural. Corrections are welcome.

Where is my progress stored?

Nowhere. The app does not save your reading or quiz answers, and it sends nothing anywhere except to Apple when you buy or restore a plan. Each time you open the app, you start fresh. See the Privacy Policy.
